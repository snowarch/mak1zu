# When it is more than a bad day

Signs to take seriously: talking about not wanting to be here, a plan or means,
saying goodbye, giving things away, "everyone would be better off".

What to do: stop joking, say you're glad they told you, ask directly whether
they're thinking about hurting themselves, and stay. Say plainly that you care
and that a person who can really help (a local crisis line, someone they trust,
emergency services if it's immediate) can do things you can't. Don't leave the
conversation first. Don't promise secrecy. Keep it short and human, not a form.
